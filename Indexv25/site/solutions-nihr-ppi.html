NIHR PPI Package.

A fully-costed Patient and Public Involvement partnership for NIHR-funded research, built on the NIHR INCLUDE framework, with community-partner MOUs already in place.

What this is

A costed, INCLUDE-aligned PPI partnership dropped into your NIHR grant, delivered from an already-existing community-partner network.

Who it is for

Academic Principal Investigators writing NIHR grants or running NIHR-funded studies that must satisfy INCLUDE.

The engagement
Grant-aligned
Duration follows your NIHR grant timeline. Scoped from single-workstream to multi-workstream, multi-community.

Why this exists

The NIHR INCLUDE framework requires funded research to demonstrate genuine Patient and Public Involvement, structured, costed, and aligned to the populations the research is meant to serve. Reviewers now flag applications where PPI reads as an afterthought or a template.

The bottleneck for most academic PIs is not intent, it is time. Building community partnerships from scratch, negotiating MOUs, and paying participants at NIHR guide rates is months of unfunded work before the grant is even submitted.

The NIHR PPI Package drops a costed, INCLUDE-aligned PPI partnership into your grant, using community partners already inside the Equity Engine panel. You do the science; we bring the community relationships.

What is included

  • INCLUDE-aligned PPI plan, drop-in ready for your grant application
  • Community-partner introductions, matched to your target populations
  • Signed MOUs with community organisations before your grant submission
  • Participant payments at NIHR guide rates, budgeted transparently
  • PPI meeting facilitation across the grant lifecycle
  • Written record of PPI decisions and how they influenced the study
  • End-of-grant PPI report for your NIHR return

What is not included

  • Full trial-management responsibility, we deliver the PPI layer, you deliver the trial
  • Publication co-authorship claims, we support your team's writing, credit is yours

Scope tiers

Single workstream. One community, one PPI workstream, one grant submission. Ideal for a Research for Patient Benefit or NIHR Fellowship application.

Multi-workstream, single community. Two or three parallel workstreams, single grant. Typical for a Programme Development Grant.

Multi-workstream, multi-community. For programme grants or platform trials with 4+ workstreams and community input required across multiple demographics.

Academic and non-profit access

Academic dataset access, for secondary analysis unrelated to the PPI package, is available at cost. Non-profit access is free by application. Both routes are subject to Trust & Ethics Board review.
