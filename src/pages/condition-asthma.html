What you can ask for

  1. A free panel count, the same working day. How many members with asthma we hold near your UK sites, by postcode district, and how they break down. Free panel count
  2. Patient involvement, before it's final. Members with asthma review your protocol, participant information sheet, consent form or product, and you get a report of what changed. Patient involvement
  3. Referrals, followed through. Members who pass your screening questions and say yes are referred to the site, and we follow each one to randomisation. Clinical trial recruitment in the UK
  4. Ask our data, then members live. An answer straight away from what members have already told us, linked to the members who gave it. Ask our data
