Type 2 diabetes in the UK

Almost 6 million

people in the UK are living with diabetes, including an estimated 1.3 million with type 2 diabetes who have not yet been diagnosed. Around 90% of people with a diagnosis have type 2.

Diabetes UK, How many people in the UK have diabetes? (2024-25 data).

2 to 4 times

People of Asian (including Indian, Pakistani and Bangladeshi), Chinese, Black African and Black Caribbean ethnicity have been found to be two to four times more likely to have diabetes than White people.

Diabetes UK, How many people in the UK have diabetes?

From 25

The NHS counts people from a South Asian, Chinese, Black African or Black Caribbean background as at higher risk of type 2 diabetes from age 25, and White people from age 40.

NHS, Symptoms of type 2 diabetes and how it is diagnosed, reviewed February 2025.
