What you can ask for

  1. Free patient feasibility, the same working day. How many members with breast cancer near your UK sites match and have already said yes, by postcode district, and how they break down. Pre-screened, never eligible. Free patient feasibility
  2. Patient engagement, before the ethics submission. Members with breast cancer review your protocol, participant information sheet, consent form or product, and you get a report of what changed. Patient engagement
  3. Referrals, followed through. Members who pass your screening questions and say yes are referred through the site's own referral link, and we follow each one to randomisation. Clinical trial recruitment in the UK
  4. Ask our data, then ask members live. An answer straight away from what members have already told us, linked to the members who gave it. Ask our data
An example question

"Is our participant information sheet clear to people living with breast cancer?"

What comes back

Members with breast cancer review it inside our platform, where download and screenshots are blocked, and comment point by point. You get every point raised, how many raised it and who, and once you've decided, a report of what you changed and what you didn't, and why.
