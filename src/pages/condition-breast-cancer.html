What you can ask for

  1. A free panel count, the same working day. How many members with breast cancer we hold near your UK sites, by postcode district, and how they break down. Free panel count
  2. Patient involvement, before it's final. Members with breast cancer review your protocol, participant information sheet, consent form or product, and you get a report of what changed. Patient involvement

What comes back

Members with breast cancer review it inside our platform, where download and screenshots are blocked, and comment point by point. You get every point raised, how many raised it and who, and once you've decided, a report of what you changed and what you didn't, and why.
