Full involvement starts at £25,000 for one clinical trial plan or one product. We'll quote a fixed price before anything starts, based on how many documents, members and questions you need. Video diaries, voice notes and interviews can be added to any piece of work and are priced in your quote. Programmes across several clinical trials or products, and annual arrangements, are quoted on request.

What members are paid

About £2,250 of a £7,500 pilot goes to members. About £4,100 of a £25,000 piece of work. Between 15p and 30p of every £1 you spend reaches the people who did the work. Members are paid £27.50 an hour, the NIHR rate, for the time each task takes. See the full rate card.

What the pilot leads to

The pilot price and the price of the next step are agreed before the pilot starts, so there are no surprises. The next step is optional.
