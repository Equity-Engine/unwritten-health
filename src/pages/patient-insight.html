What changed, in our pilot

People living with the condition reshaped a clinical trial programme's recruitment strategy and consent materials.

Both were changed because of what they told us. The programme went on to enrol 560 people, 112% of target. Their input was gathered for the pilot specifically, and their work was the recruitment strategy and consent materials, not the enrolment. The sponsor can't be named.

Advisory boards give you a handful of voices

Advisory boards and roundtables give you a handful of voices a few times a year. We give you people living with the condition, built to match everyone who has it, whenever you have a question, and a report showing who said what and what changed. It sits alongside your advocacy partnerships rather than replacing them.
