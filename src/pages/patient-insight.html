An example question

"What does a bad week with psoriasis look like, and is it different for people in the most deprived areas?"

What comes back

An answer straight away from what members with psoriasis have already told us, broken down by deprivation and linked to the members who said it. Then ask members live: survey answers, voice notes and video diaries within hours or days, with the themes, how many raised each one and who, and de-identified quotes.

What changed, in our 560 pilot

Before the protocol was final, people of African descent reshaped a phase 2 clinical trial's eligibility criteria, visits, sites and consent materials.

The programme enrolled 560 people in 12 weeks, 112% of target. Their work was on the design and the materials, not the enrolment; we don't claim it caused the result, and we don't promise it for anyone else. Done by hand, before the platform. The sponsor can't be named.

Advisory boards give you a handful of voices

Advisory boards and roundtables give you a handful of voices a few times a year. We give you people living with the condition, built from the communities clinical trials miss, whenever you have a question, and a report showing who said what and what changed. It sits alongside your advocacy partnerships rather than replacing them.

Evidence, never brand work

Members never promote a product, act as ambassadors or mentors, or appear in a company's marketing.
