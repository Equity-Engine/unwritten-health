COPD in the UK

1.2 million

people in the UK have a COPD diagnosis, and Asthma + Lung UK estimates around 600,000 more are living with COPD undiagnosed.

NICE, January 2026; Asthma + Lung UK, What is COPD?, reviewed May 2025.

More deprived areas

COPD remains more common in areas with older and more deprived populations, reflecting historical smoking, workplace risks and air pollution.

Chief Medical Officer for England, Health trends and variation in England, September 2025.
