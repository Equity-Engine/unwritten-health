Eczema in the UK

1 in 5 and 1 in 10

Eczema affects at least 1 in 5 children and 1 in 10 adults in the UK.

Eczema UK (formerly the National Eczema Society).

Not always red

Atopic eczema can make skin red, white, purple or grey, or lighter or darker than the skin around it, depending on skin tone. On brown or black skin, redness may be harder to see.

NHS, Atopic eczema, reviewed September 2024; British Association of Dermatologists, Atopic eczema, August 2025.
