Lung cancer in the UK

Around 50,200

people are diagnosed with lung cancer in the UK every year, nearly 140 a day. It is the third most common cancer in the UK.

Cancer Research UK, lung cancer incidence statistics (2019, 2021 to 2022).

37%

of lung cancer cases in the UK are associated with deprivation: around 18,300 more cases a year than if every area had the rates of the least deprived.

Cancer Research UK, UK cancer incidence by deprivation 2019 to 2022, September 2026.

By 2030

The NHS Lung Cancer Screening Programme, launched in 2019 in the areas hardest hit by lung cancer, has reached half of England's most at-risk people and is due to be rolled out across England by 2030.

NHS England, May 2026; DHSC and NHS England, National Cancer Plan for England, September 2026.
