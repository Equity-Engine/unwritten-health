What it is, and isn't

When a submission needs patient-reported burden, treatment acceptability or the experience of a particular group, we ask members through surveys, video diaries and voice notes and break the answers down by age, sex, ethnicity and deprivation. It's patient-reported data collected for your question, written up as an appendix or as briefing for patient experts. It isn't medical records data, and every report says so. A survey of 300 members with video diaries is quoted per piece of work.
