Who it's for · Clinical development and recruitment

For whoever owns UK recruitment for a clinical trial.

At a sponsor, a CRO with UK sites, or an academic clinical trials unit, in the UK or anywhere else. We're built from the communities clinical trials miss: for the people your sites can't reach, there's us. And we follow every referral through, and tell you which ones went nowhere and why.

What you'd use us for

  • Patient feasibility: how many members near your sites match and have already said yes, with the breakdown, the same working day.
  • Site choice by postcode district: where people with the condition live, and what would make taking part possible.
  • Invitation testing: your protocol synopsis, information sheet, invitation and visit plan, reviewed by people who live with the condition before the ethics submission.
  • Referrals: members who pass your screening questions and say yes to your clinical trial, through the site's own referral link, with a best time to call.
  • Every patient followed to randomisation: one line per patient each week, randomisation checked against your own record, and the site hears first about anyone not yet reached, with its own note beside them. It's a record of where each patient is, never a score for the site.
  • Evidence for your Inclusion and Diversity Plan: questions 1, 3 and 4, answered with real people near your sites.

The evidence for your Inclusion and Diversity Plan

Representation is a clinical operations question now, not a corporate responsibility budget. Patient feasibility answers questions 1, 3 and 4 of the HRA and MHRA plan with real people near your sites: who is affected by the condition, what your recruitment goals are, and how you'll recruit and retain them.

"Representative" is the goal for your clinical trial, not a description of our membership: we help a clinical trial become representative by supplying the people it's missing.

An example question

"How many people with COPD have joined you near our sites in Leeds, Leicester and Glasgow, and how many have opted in to hear about clinical trials?"

What comes back

Free patient feasibility, the same working day: how many members with COPD match near each site, by postcode district, broken down by age, sex, ethnicity and deprivation, and how many have opted in to hear about clinical trials. Real people who've said yes, not a forecast of enrolment, and pre-screened, never eligible.

Sponsors and CROs

If your CRO drafts the documents, the CRO can send them to us and receive the report. Global protocols arrive fixed: what the UK team owns is the UK information sheet, the invitation, the site list and the recruitment plan, and that's where we work.

Academic clinical trials units

Our members are people living with the condition near where you're recruiting. We quote the whole clinical trial at one set price before anything starts, so it fits inside a grant, and universities usually can't recover VAT, so every quote shows the figure with VAT as well as without.

Already have a PPI group?

Keep it. A group of ten can't tell you how a thousand people with the condition would read your information sheet. We give you the breakdown by who said it, and the same people can then be recruited.

First-in-human and rare disease

If your first clinical trial is in healthy volunteers, say so when you ask for patient feasibility and we'll tell you honestly whether our members can help. If it's in patients, the information sheet and the visit burden are exactly what members are good at.

For a condition with a few hundred UK patients, we don't pretend to hold 150 of them. We build a small group to order, patients or caregivers, and we'll report on a group as small as ten. Recruiting usually takes about four weeks, and the build is part of your quote, not charged on top.

Safety

Members are told when they join how to report a side effect to their doctor and to the MHRA's Yellow Card scheme. Every survey answer, voice note and video diary is screened by our team before it reaches you. Anything that mentions a side effect, a product complaint or a safety concern about a named medicine is passed to your pharmacovigilance contact within one working day, and we close every piece of work with a reconciliation log of what was passed on and when.

Fees tied to agreed deliverables

Fees per randomised participant are checked against the site's own record and capped, with a credit or refund if we fall short of the referrals we commit to. The arrangement sits in the contract and in the recruitment arrangements the ethics committee sees; eligibility, consent and randomisation stay the site's. The first clinical trial with a new client runs as a fixed-price pilot agreed jointly.

What we don't claim

That following patients up raises enrolment: we haven't found controlled evidence that it does. It gives the team the facts, not a promised result. And that our members represent every person with a condition: we're built from the gap on purpose, and we say so.
