Reshaping a clinical trial invitation.

Members reshaped the invitation for a programme that went on to enrol 560 people, 112% of target, by March 2026.

What members did, and what they didn't

Their work was the invitation, not the enrolment. The enrolment is the sponsor's, and many things went into it. We don't claim our members' work caused it, and we don't promise it for anyone else.
