Case study · Our pilot

Reshaping a clinical trial's recruitment strategy and consent materials.

In our pilot, people living with the condition reshaped the recruitment strategy and consent materials for a clinical trial programme that went on to enrol 560 people, 112% of target.

What changed

Two things changed because of what people living with the condition told us: the recruitment strategy and the consent materials.

This was our pilot. Their input was gathered for the pilot specifically.

What they did, and what they didn't

Their work was the recruitment strategy and consent materials, not the enrolment. The enrolment is the sponsor's, and many things went into it. We don't claim their work caused it, and we don't promise it for anyone else.

The sponsor can't be named.
