Every member has finished onboarding and passed our sign-up checks, and conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions.

Where we're short. Our prostate cancer members aren't there yet: Black men are about twice as likely to get prostate cancer as white men, and our prostate cancer members are 3% Black. We're recruiting to close the gap. The free panel count tells you exactly who we hold today.

Prostate cancer in the UK

64,000+

men are diagnosed with prostate cancer every year, and it is the most common cancer in men in the UK.

Prostate Cancer UK, Facts and figures, January 2026 (NHS figures for 2022); Cancer Research UK.

1 in 4

Black men in the UK will get prostate cancer in their lifetime, against about 1 in 8 men overall.

Prostate Cancer UK, Are you at risk?, December 2024.
