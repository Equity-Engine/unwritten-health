Our members

15,000 people in the UK living with long-term conditions have joined us.

November 2026, and growing daily, and we're expanding into Germany, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Portugal, Ireland and Poland. We're built from the communities clinical trials miss: people from ethnic minority backgrounds, the most deprived areas and rural areas, older people, people with more than one condition, and people who don't read English. We engage members continuously and in the moment, so we know what each member told us before, at their stage of the condition.

Members join through Equity Engine{{EXT}}, our member platform, built for under-served communities from day one and run by Unwritten Health. Conditions are self-reported, with qualifying questions, and each member has told us whether they'd like to hear about clinical trials. The same members do patient engagement and clinical trial recruitment in the UK.

What members are told

People join us through sources they already trust, so keeping that trust is our job. This is what every member is promised, in the welcome and in every project notice.

Who we are and who we work with

Companies and researchers work with us to ask you questions, and, if you've said yes to hearing about them, to tell you about clinical trials near you.

Who sees what

Your name stays with us. A company never gets it. If you say yes to a clinical trial, the research team at that site gets your details so they can call you, and only then.

What we never do

We never sell your details. They're never used for marketing or insurance.

Saying no changes nothing

You can say no to any question or any clinical trial, and you can leave whenever you like. When you leave, your recordings are deleted.

After a referral

We check in with you after a week and after your visit. If nobody has called you, we tell the site. We never chase you.

Your community

What members say goes back to the groups you joined through, as numbers and themes, never by name, so your community sees what changed.

The AI

A tool reads what members have told us, under published rules, and a person checks anything before a company sees it. It never invents a patient.

No labels

Nobody is told they're under-served, hard to reach or anything else. You're a person living with a condition, and that's why we ask you.

Active, not just registered

Every one of the 15,000 has been active in the last three months, and 70% of members answer when asked. Simple questions come back in hours, complex ones in days.

Every member has also finished onboarding and passed our sign-up checks: bot defence, network risk checks, email checks and device checks, with anything flagged reviewed by a person. Someone who started signing up and didn't finish isn't one of them.

How they joined. Every one through community outreach; we've never run paid search. We started in March 2026 through Equity Engine, with a head start from the community work behind our founder's previous company. Since then we've grown by going to people: through patient groups and community organisations, at community hubs and health fairs, in person as well as online.

Members tell us things through short in-the-moment check-ins, voice notes, video diaries, secure on-screen review (download and screenshots blocked), and live interviews and groups. Equity Engine also gives them community and content, which keeps them active.

What we don't claim

  • That our members represent every person with a condition. We're built from the gap on purpose, and we say so.
  • That we can verify ethnicity or diagnosis. Both are self-reported; the site confirms diagnosis. If you need a stricter screen, we'll screen to your criteria.
  • We don't currently hold disability, skin tone, dexterity or device experience as standing fields. We can screen for them on request.

The rules we keep

  • Two consents before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial. The site's informed consent is a third step, and never ours.
  • Sponsors never learn who members are: only we, and the site a member says yes to, know their name. We never sell member data.
  • Members can say no to any question or any clinical trial, and leave whenever they like.
  • Members are 18 or over.
  • Members sign a confidentiality agreement when they join and are trained to understand they're reviewing material they don't own.
  • After every piece of work, members get a short summary of what changed because of them.
