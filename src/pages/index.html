The panel

13,050 people across the UK living with long-term conditions.

Self-declared, October 2026, and every one active in the last three months. Each has told us whether they'd consider a clinical trial.

1,900 of our members live with type 2 diabetes, representative of the people who live with it by age, sex and ethnicity, including the communities clinical trials usually miss.

Type 2 diabetes is two to four times more likely in people of South Asian, African-Caribbean and Black African descent. Of our 1,900 members with it, 16% are Asian and 5% are Black, against 9% and 4% of the population of England and Wales. Where we're short of a condition, the panel page says so.

The gap

Where clinical trials lose people.

4 in 10

Of NIHR-funded clinical trials reported from 2017 to 2020, nearly 4 in 10 didn't reach their original recruitment target, and 37% needed more time.

Jacques et al., BMJ Open, 2022.

30% and 45%

In a 2017 Tufts survey of 2,000 doctors and nurses, mainly in the US and Europe, nearly 30% of doctors and 45% of nurses said they never heard back after referring a patient to a clinical trial.

Tufts CSDD, in Applied Clinical Trials, January 2017.

90,000

From 2013 to 2018, the areas of England with the most long-term illness had the fewest people taking part in research. To match, about 90,000 people would have had to be recruited in different areas.

NIHR Evidence, on Bower et al., September 2020.

"Not normally"

The HRA expects public involvement in all research. Participant information developed without it "will not normally receive a favourable opinion" from an NHS ethics committee.

HRA, public involvement guidance.

We don't claim that following patients up raises enrolment: we haven't found controlled evidence that it does. It gives the team the facts, not a promised result.

Trust

How we treat members.

Two consents.

Before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial.

Paid for their time.

£27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys. Never for joining a clinical trial, and the check-ins after a referral are unpaid.

Honest about what we know.

Conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions. Eligibility is always the site's call.
