What you can ask for

  1. A free panel count, the same working day. How many members with obesity we hold near your UK sites, by postcode district, and how they break down. Free panel count
  2. Patient involvement, before it's final. Members with obesity review your protocol, participant information sheet, consent form or product, and you get a report of what changed. Patient involvement
