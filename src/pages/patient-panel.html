The panel

13,050* people living with long-term conditions. Every one active.

Our panel runs on Equity Engine{{EXT}}, our member platform, owned and run by Unwritten Health. Conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions, and each member has told us whether they'd consider a clinical trial. The same panel does patient involvement and clinical trial recruitment in the UK.

Active, not registered

Every one of our 13,050* members has answered something in the last three months. We count activity, not sign-ups: someone who registered and went quiet isn't in that number.

*Self-declared, October 2026.

Where they came from: we started building the panel in March 2026 through Equity Engine{{EXT}}, our community platform, recruiting online and through community partners, with a head start from the community work behind our founder's previous company. Members are paid for their time from their first survey, at the rates below, so nobody is asked to give their time for free.

Simple questions come back in hours. Complex ones in days.

Where we're short. The type 2 diabetes, obesity and COPD panels are close to who has the condition. The cancer panels and depression are not there yet: Black men are about twice as likely to get prostate cancer as white men, and our prostate cancer panel is 3% Black; depression is 5% Asian and 3% Black, below the population, never mind the condition. We're recruiting to close those gaps and will update this table as we do. If your work is in one of those conditions, the free panel check tells you exactly who we hold today.

What we don't claim

  • Conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions. If you need a stricter screen, we'll screen to your criteria.
  • Members chose to join. The panel is built to reflect the breakdown of each condition, but it doesn't represent every person with that condition.
  • We don't currently hold disability, skin tone, dexterity or device experience as standing fields. We can screen for them on request.

How members are paid

Members are paid £27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys. Pay is worked out for the time a task takes and rounded up to the nearest 50p.

Members earn points (100 points is £1) and swap them for e-vouchers from £5, including a supermarket option.

The rules we keep:

  • Members are paid for their time, never for their answers.
  • Members are never paid to join a clinical trial, and the check-ins after a referral are unpaid.
  • Two consents before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial.
  • Members see what a task pays before they start it.
  • Points don't expire while a member is active.
  • We only hold back points where there's clear evidence of fraud.
  • No member earns more than £1,000 in a tax year.
  • Members are 18 or over.
  • Clients never pay members directly and never learn who they are. We never sell members' data.

Members sign a confidentiality agreement when they join and are trained to understand they're reviewing material they don't own. After every piece of work, they get a short summary of what changed because of them.
