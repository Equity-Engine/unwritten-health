Invented example · invented numbers

"How many of your members with asthma on a high-dose inhaled steroid, who've had two or more oral steroid courses in the last year, live within 15 miles of Manchester, and what would stop them joining a 12-week placebo-controlled clinical trial?"

From our data, straight away

214 members match on treatment and area; 161 have opted in to hear about clinical trials. Asked last year what would stop them, the three most common answers were the chance of a "dummy medicine" (placebo) (38%), weekday visits (31%) and travel (22%). Here are the 19 voice notes those answers came from, with who said each.

From members, two days later

87 answered. 54 would consider it if visits could be early morning or Saturday; 23 said no, mostly because of the placebo.
