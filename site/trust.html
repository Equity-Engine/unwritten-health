Trust

How we handle your material, and our members.

Lines we won't cross

These hold whoever is buying and whatever they'd pay.

  1. No brand work. Members never promote a product, act as ambassadors or mentors, or appear in a company's marketing.
  2. No selling identities. Sponsors never learn who members are: only we, and the site a member says yes to, know their name. We never sell member data.
  3. No pay for joining. Members are paid £27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys. They are never paid for joining a clinical trial, and the check-ins after a referral are unpaid.
  4. No synthetic patients. Every answer, including the AI's, traces back to real members.
  5. Two consents before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial. The site's informed consent is a third step, and never ours.

Your material

  • Members sign a confidentiality agreement when they join, and can sign your own wording before seeing your material.
  • Material is shown inside our platform and never sent out. Download and browser screenshots are blocked. The confidentiality agreement every member signs covers photographing the screen, and material is taken down when the work ends.
  • Members are trained, when they join, that they're reviewing material they don't own.
  • You can exclude specific questions or comments from the report.

Safety and complaints

  • Members are told when they join how to report a side effect to their doctor and to the MHRA's Yellow Card scheme.
  • Every survey answer, voice note and video diary is screened by our team before it reaches a client.
  • Anything that mentions a side effect, a product complaint or a safety concern about a named medicine or device is passed to the client's named safety contact within one working day.
  • Every piece of work closes with a reconciliation log of what was passed on and when.

Your data and theirs

  • Our AI is built only on what our members have told us: no synthetic patients and no outside data filling the gaps. Every answer links to the members' own answers it came from, and shows who gave them, so you can check it rather than trust it. It runs under our own framework that sets what it may and may not do.
  • We never sell members' data, and we never license anyone's answers to train AI.
  • Your material and members' words stay yours and theirs. Only anonymised patterns across many pieces of work are kept, and you can opt out in your contract.
  • Reports never name members or include anything that could identify them. We never share members' video diaries or voice notes. You get the themes and de-identified quotes.

Our members

  • Two consents before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial. The site's informed consent is a third step, and never ours.
  • Members are paid £27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys. Never for their answers, never for joining a clinical trial, and the check-ins after a referral are unpaid.
  • Clients never pay members directly. Sponsors never learn who members are: only we, and the site a member says yes to, know their name.
  • Members see what a task pays before they start, and swap points for e-vouchers from £5.
  • Members see a summary of what changed because of them after every piece of work.
  • Members reading material about their own condition can stop at any time.

What we never claim

  • That involvement will improve your recruitment, uptake or outcomes. We report what members said and what you changed.
  • That a report guarantees approval from anyone.
  • That following patients up after a referral raises enrolment. We report where each referral stands and why anyone stopped.
  • That a referral means a member can take part. A referral means they passed your screening questions; eligibility is the site's call.
  • That members' conditions are confirmed against medical records.
  • That our members represent everyone with a condition.
