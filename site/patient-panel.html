Our members

13,050 people across the UK living with long-term conditions have joined us.

As of October 2026, and growing daily. We count a member as active once they've finished onboarding and passed our sign-up checks, and 70% of members answer when asked. We go back to the same people again and again, so we know what each member told us before, at their stage of the condition. Members use Equity Engine (opens in a new tab), our member platform, owned and run by Unwritten Health. Conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions, and each member has told us whether they'd consider a clinical trial. The same members do patient involvement and clinical trial recruitment in the UK.

Active, not just registered

A member counts as active once they've finished onboarding and passed our sign-up checks: bot defence, network risk checks, email checks and device checks, with anything flagged reviewed by a person. Someone who started signing up and didn't finish isn't in our 13,050.

13,050 as of October 2026, and growing daily. Conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions.

Where they came from: we started in March 2026 through Equity Engine (opens in a new tab), our community platform, with a head start from the community work behind our founder's previous company. More than 3,500 had joined by the June 2026 launch. Since then we've grown by going to people: we present Equity Engine at community hubs across the UK, attend health fairs and spend time in the community. Members are paid for their time from their first survey, at the rates below, so nobody is asked to give their time for free.

70% of members answer when asked. Simple questions come back in hours, complex ones in days.

Built to match everyone who has the condition

For each condition, we recruit to match everyone who has it, using published data on the condition, rather than taking whoever turns up and adjusting the numbers afterwards. We show the breakdown, and we publish where we're short. Here are our members today, condition by condition.

What we know about each member

Their condition, in their own words, and qualifying questions about it. Their experience of it: treatment, and how it affects daily life. Whether they'd consider a clinical trial. And their background:

  • Ethnicity
  • Gender
  • Sex
  • Sexuality
  • Age
  • Other conditions they live with
  • Household income
  • Deprivation (from their postcode)
  • Rural or urban (from the first part of the postcode)
  • Education
  • First language

Members tell us things through surveys, voice notes, video diaries, secure on-screen review, and live interviews and groups. Equity Engine also gives them community and content, which keeps them coming back.

What we don't claim

  • Conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions. If you need a stricter screen, we'll screen to your criteria.
  • Members chose to join. We build to match the breakdown of each condition, but our members don't represent every person with that condition.
  • We don't currently hold disability, skin tone, dexterity or device experience as standing fields. We can screen for them on request.

How members are paid

Members are paid £27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys. Pay is worked out for the time a task takes and rounded up to the nearest 50p.

Members earn points (100 points is £1) and swap them for e-vouchers from £5, including a supermarket option.

The rules we keep:

  • Members are paid for their time, never for their answers.
  • Members are never paid to join a clinical trial, and the check-ins after a referral are unpaid.
  • Two consents before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial. The site's informed consent is a third step, and never ours.
  • Members see what a task pays before they start it.
  • Points don't expire while a member is active.
  • We only hold back points where there's clear evidence of fraud.
  • No member earns more than £1,000 in a tax year.
  • Members are 18 or over.
  • Clients never pay members directly. Sponsors never learn who members are: only we, and the site a member says yes to, know their name. We never sell member data.

Members sign a confidentiality agreement when they join and are trained to understand they're reviewing material they don't own. After every piece of work, they get a short summary of what changed because of them.

Source: NIHR payment rates for public involvement, updated 16 December 2025.
