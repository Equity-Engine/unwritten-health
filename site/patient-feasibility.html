Free patient feasibility

How many patients near your sites have already said yes?

Site feasibility asks the sites to estimate how many patients they could find. We ask the patients themselves. Tell us the condition and where your sites are, and we'll reply the same working day with how many members near each site match, by condition, stage and postcode district, how many have already opted in to hear about clinical trials, and how they break down by ethnicity, age, sex and area deprivation.

Free, and no sales call needed.

  • Pre-screened, never eligible. The numbers are members who match on what they've told us. Your site confirms eligibility.
  • Where we're short, we say so, and say how we'd build it through the patient groups, community organisations and GP practices people already trust. Low numbers today are where the work starts, not a no.
  • For your Inclusion and Diversity Plan. The numbers answer questions 1, 3 and 4 of the HRA and MHRA plan with real people near your sites.

Invented example · invented numbers

"How many of your members with asthma on a high-dose inhaled steroid, who've had two or more oral steroid courses in the last year, live within 15 miles of Manchester, and what would stop them joining a 12-week placebo-controlled clinical trial?"

From our data, straight away

214 members match on treatment and area; 161 have opted in to hear about clinical trials. Asked last year what would stop them, the three most common answers were the chance of a "dummy medicine" (placebo) (38%), weekday visits (31%) and travel (22%). Here are the 19 voice notes those answers came from, with who said each.

From members, two days later

87 answered. 54 would consider it if visits could be early morning or Saturday; 23 said no, mostly because of the placebo.
