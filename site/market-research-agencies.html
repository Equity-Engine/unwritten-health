Who it's for · Market research agencies

The right patients for your pharma clients.

13,050 people across the UK living with long-term conditions have joined us (as of October 2026, and growing daily), each counted once they've finished onboarding and passed our sign-up checks.

What you'd use us for

  • Incidence by condition: counts by condition, and near the areas you name, the same working day.
  • The breakdown for each condition, and where we're short, published.
  • Fraud and quality checks on every member, described below.
An example question

"What's the incidence of eczema among your members, and how many fit our screener for a 20-minute survey?"

What comes back

The same working day: incidence by condition among our members, and how many fit your screener. Every member has passed our sign-up checks against bots and fake accounts, and conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions.

Fraud and quality checks

Every new member goes through sign-up checks to keep out bots and fake accounts: bot defence, network risk checks, two-stage email checks, privacy-respecting device checks and an optional phone check. Extra steps appear only when something looks wrong, and flagged sign-ups go to a person for review. We count a member only once they've finished onboarding and passed these checks, and conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions.

Members stay ours

Members are never passed to you by name. Sponsors and agencies never learn who members are.

Paid for their time

Members are paid £27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys.
