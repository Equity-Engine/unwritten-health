The problems we solve

Designed with too little from the people they're for. Then patients lost after the referral.

Clinical trials and products get designed with too little from the people they're for, and once recruitment starts, sponsors lose sight of patients after the referral. The result is evidence that doesn't hold up, recruitment that misses target, and spend nobody can trace.

Protocols, consent forms and products are finalised before people with the condition have had a real say.

Clinical trials: finding people

Clinical trials miss recruitment targets, and research isn't where the illness is.

Of NIHR-funded clinical trials reported 2017 to 2020, nearly 4 in 10 missed their original target. From 2013 to 2018, the areas of England with the most long-term illness had the fewest people taking part in research.

Jacques et al., BMJ Open, 2022; NIHR Evidence.

How we work

Ask our data, then ask members live.

Our AI is built only on what our members have told us: no synthetic patients and no outside data filling the gaps. Every answer links to the members' own answers it came from, and shows who gave them, so you can check it rather than trust it. It runs under our own framework that sets what it may and may not do.

Our members

13,050 people across the UK living with long-term conditions have joined us.

As of October 2026. Every one active in the last three months, and 70% of members answer when asked. Simple questions come back in hours, complex ones in days.

1,900 of our members live with type 2 diabetes. Type 2 diabetes is two to four times more likely in people of South Asian, African-Caribbean and Black African descent. Of our 1,900 members with it, 16% are Asian and 5% are Black, against 9% and 4% of the population of England and Wales. Where we're short of a condition, our members page says so.

Trust

Lines we won't cross.

  • No brand work. Members never promote a product, act as ambassadors or mentors, or appear in a company's marketing.
  • No selling identities. Sponsors never learn who members are: only we, and the site a member says yes to, know their name. We never sell member data.
  • No pay for joining. Members are paid £27.50 an hour, in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement, for answering our surveys. Never for joining a clinical trial, and the check-ins after a referral are unpaid.
  • No synthetic patients. Every answer, including the AI's, traces back to real members.
  • Two consents before any detail leaves us: a standing opt-in to hear about clinical trials, then a yes to one named clinical trial. The site's informed consent is a third step, and never ours.
