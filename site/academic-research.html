Academic research

For clinical trials units and NIHR researchers.

Clinical trial recruitment in the UK for trials units, and patient involvement for NIHR researchers. Recruiting for a clinical trial, or involving people in an application or funded research? Our members can do both.

Referrals

Referrals your clinical trial manager can follow.

Members who pass your screening questions and say yes are referred to the site, with a best time to call. We check in with every one, and you get a line a week per referral: where the patient says things stand, the site's note beside it, and why anyone stopped.

Eligibility stays the site's call. We don't promise enrolment.

Patient involvement

Add the people your involvement group is missing.

Your PPI group does the work the UK Standards for Public Involvement ask for: shaping the question, sitting on the oversight group, staying with the research. What it usually can't do is reach enough people with the condition to show whether a point comes from one group or from everyone. That's what we add, alongside your group and your public co-applicant, not instead of them.

For clinical trials units: patient recruitment

Members who pass your screening questions and say yes are referred to the site, with a best time to call. Anyone the site hasn't reached by day 7 goes to the site that week, then to your report a week later if still not reached. On Be Part of Research, research teams report how many volunteers were interested and how many took part. We add the patient's own account of where things stand, with the site's note beside it.

For NIHR applicants: the one-document step

Send one document, a lay summary or a draft information sheet. Ten members who live with the condition review it inside our platform. You get a short report: who they were, what they raised, what you changed. It's written for the involvement and inclusion sections of the application, and it says what it is: a consultation with members, to sit next to the involvement you describe elsewhere. It usually takes about ten working days from sending the document to receiving the report, and we tell you the exact number before you pay. Invoiced or paid by card.

For funded research: the advisory add-on

Members join your advisory or steering group as named, ongoing contributors, with their consent, paid in line with NIHR's rate for public involvement for the life of the research. We recruit, brief and support them; you work with them as you would any public contributor. That's involvement in the sense the Standards mean, and it's the rung most PPI leads asked us for.

For clinical trials units and infrastructure

If you'd cost us into applications across a unit or a centre, we'll agree a rate per document and put it in writing, ex-VAT, so your business manager sees the real number. Ask us.

Costing

Universities usually can't recover VAT, so every quote we send shows the figure with VAT as well as without. We say that here so nobody finds it on the invoice.
