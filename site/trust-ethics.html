Trust & ethics

Trust is not optional in a data business. It belongs on the homepage. And on this one.

The Equity Engine is governed by consent, community-partnership, an independent Trust & Ethics Board with veto power, and UK data anchoring.

The four pillars
01

Consented at every stage.

Every participant re-consents on a use-case-specific schema, at NIHR guide rates. Consent is not a one-time sign-up event, it is per-use.

  • Sign-up consent covers panel membership and SDoH profiling
  • Every study, survey or council use requires a fresh, specific consent
  • Consent language is co-developed with community partners
  • Participants can withdraw specific consents without leaving the panel
  • All consent records are auditable
02

Community-partnered.

Every participant is recruited through a named community organisation under a signed memorandum of understanding, not through a digital panel aggregator.

  • Community partners share in sponsor engagement fees
  • Community partners hold veto power on any output that identifies their community
  • Panel refresh cycles are agreed with community partners, not imposed
  • Community partners are named in the Trust & Ethics Board
03

Independently governed.

Our Trust & Ethics Board sits outside operational decision-making. It reviews all outputs before release and holds veto power over any output that would identify a community.

  • Lay chair, not the founder, not the CEO
  • Clinical ethicist with regulated healthcare experience
  • Data-protection officer with UK GDPR expertise
  • Two community-partner representatives with output veto
  • Quarterly review of all major outputs
04

UK-anchored.

Data stays in UK jurisdiction. Every output is auditable to raw response level. Sub-processors are UK-only unless the participant has explicitly consented to specific overseas processing.

  • UK-based hosting, UK-based analytical processing
  • UK GDPR Article 30 record of processing activities maintained
  • Raw response-level audit trail on every dataset
  • Sub-processor list is published and updated
Sign-up profile

Every participant is profiled on eight social factors at sign-up.

That is the mechanism that lets us produce subgroup evidence at all. The eight factors below are aligned to MHRA IDP, NIHR INCLUDE and EMA regulatory expectations and cover the dimensions regulators, HTA bodies and NHS procurement now ask about first.

None of these factors are inferred. All eight are consented and self-reported at panel sign-up, and can be updated by the participant at any time.

Governance

The Trust & Ethics Board.

Independent of operations. Reviews all major outputs before release. Output veto means the Board can require an output to be reworked or withheld if it would identify an individual community, breach consent scope, or create a risk to a participant, before the output ever leaves the platform.

Board being established. The seat descriptions below define the roles we are recruiting for. The Board is expected to be seated in Q4 2026. Until then, an interim advisory group performs equivalent review functions. Board composition and member biographies will be published on this page.

Lay chair

Not the founder, not the CEO. Provides the outside-in review that HTA bodies and NHS procurement now expect.

Clinical ethicist

Regulated-healthcare research ethics background. Reviews study design and consent language.

Data-protection officer

UK GDPR expertise. Reviews data handling, sub-processors and audit trails.

Community-partner representatives (×2)

Rotating community-partner voices, with formal power to require an output to be reworked or withheld. Output veto
