What's in Equity Engine MVP 2.0.

Equity Engine is the member platform behind our UK panel, owned and run by Unwritten Health. This is what shipped in MVP 2.0, in plain words. It's a record of the June 2026 launch; the panel has grown since, to 13,050 members (self-declared, counted September 2026).

For members: joining and profiles

Members register, confirm their email and are approved before they set a password. A short profile asks about their condition, age, region, ethnicity and more. They get three gentle reminders before the profile becomes required, a banner until it's nearly complete, and can edit it any time. The whole platform is available in 14 languages.

For members: surveys, points and e-vouchers

Every new member gets a three-question icebreaker on day one. New surveys are offered to members whose profile fits the survey's criteria, and answers save as they go. Each survey carries points, which members swap for e-vouchers at £5, £10, £25 or £50, delivered within seconds.

For members: community pages

Members can see how people living with the same condition answered, with more detail unlocking as more people join.

Accessible, and works on a phone

Built to WCAG 2.1 AA: skip links, visible focus, and errors and confirmations announced to screen readers. Every part of the platform, for members, partners and our team, works on a phone.

Keeping the panel real

Sign-up checks keep out bots and fake accounts: bot defence, network risk checks, two-stage email checks, privacy-respecting device checks, an optional phone check (numbers kept only as one-way hashes), and checks for university researchers. Extra steps appear only when something looks wrong. Flagged sign-ups and unusual patterns go to a person for review; nobody is turned away automatically.

For partner organisations

Charities, NHS bodies and academic partners see summary reports for the conditions they work on: themes, how people feel over time, and anonymised quotes with the member's broad group (for example, "Female, 35 to 44, North West"). Partners only ever see summaries, never anything that identifies a member, and reports download as a PDF. Academic partners get free access in exchange for citing Equity Engine, with a ready-made citation.

For our team

An approvals queue with risk signals on each sign-up, survey set-up and targeting, bulk invitations, editable profile questions, a regional view across the UK, and e-voucher fulfilment.

The founder dataset

1,204 anonymised UK community survey responses, imported with consent as the founder dataset. They're the basis of The Frequency of Exclusion (opens in a new tab). No names or contact details were kept, and the source files were destroyed after import.

How we use AI

We use AI to turn what members write into themes and a measure of how they feel. Names, emails and anything that could identify a member are removed before any text is analysed, and analysis can be re-run without processing the same answers twice.

Data and privacy

Each partner sees only the conditions it works on, enforced on our servers, not just on screen. Partners see summaries, never individual records. Imported data is anonymised, passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, credentials are rotated regularly, and the password reset never reveals who has an account. Unwritten Health is registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ZC109542).

Email

An authenticated sending domain and ten branded templates: confirmation, waitlist, invitation, password reset, survey invitations, a monthly community digest and notifications.
