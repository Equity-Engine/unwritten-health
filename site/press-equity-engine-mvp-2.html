Unwritten Health today announced the production launch of Equity Engine MVP 2.0, the member platform where people living with long-term conditions tell us about their health, their care and clinical research. The platform launched in private beta with more than 3,500 members, and brings sign-up checks against fake accounts, e-voucher payouts in seconds, and plain English throughout.

Built for the communities clinical trials usually miss

Equity Engine is designed to be a safe, simple place for people living with long-term conditions to be heard and properly counted. MVP 2.0 moves the platform from working prototype to production: live in private beta, on its own domain, with encrypted connections throughout.

Checks at the front door

Every new account now goes through a set of sign-up checks to keep out bots and fake accounts: bot defence, network risk checks, two-stage email checks, privacy-respecting device checks, an optional phone check (numbers are kept only as one-way hashes), and checks for university researchers. Extra steps appear only when something looks wrong, so honest members aren't held up, and flagged sign-ups go to a person for review.

Paid for their time, in seconds

Members earn points for answering surveys and can swap them for e-vouchers, which arrive within seconds. Voucher stock is encrypted, with safeguards against reward farming.

Plain English everywhere

The privacy policy, the terms, every email and every page have been rewritten to a reading age of 12 to 13, with UK GDPR explained in plain words.

Registered with the ICO

Unwritten Health is registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ZC109542).

"Health research has too often spoken about communities, not with them. Equity Engine MVP 2.0 is built to change that, with the security and clarity people should expect from anything that holds their health information. We're proud to be at the point where NHS bodies, charities and academic partners can rely on what comes out of the platform."Ashish Rishi, Founder, Unwritten Health

About Unwritten Health

Unwritten Health is a UK patient experience data company: patient experience data and patient recruitment, UK. 13,050 people across the UK living with long-term conditions have joined it (as of October 2026). Clients ask them questions and recruit from them, with every referral followed through. Equity Engine is its member platform. Registered in England and Wales (Company No. 16561594); ICO registration ZC109542. Based at Swan Buildings, 20 Swan Street, Manchester, M4 5JW.
