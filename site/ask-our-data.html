Two steps.

1. Ask our data

An instant answer from what members have already told us, broken down by who said it, with every answer linked to the members' own answers.

2. Ask members live

One click sends the question to members whose profile fits. Answers come back in hours or days, and 70% of members answer when asked.

The AI also reviews protocols, consent forms, recruitment strategies and other documents against what patients have told us, and shows you which answers each point came from.

Footnote, our AI framework

Ask our members anything. Every answer shows who said it.

Built only on what our members have told us. No synthetic patients. Linked to the real answers behind it, so you can check it rather than trust it.

What Footnote checks, and where it runs

  1. Our members' data only. It runs on our own ring-fenced data, with no synthetic patients and no outside data filling the gaps.
  2. Every answer shows its source. It links to the members' answers behind it and says who gave them.
  3. Fairness checks. Algorithms look for bias in its answers.
  4. Where it runs. Claude, through Amazon Bedrock in the London region, with data stored in UK data centres.
  5. Who can see member records. Only our founder and our CTO, and only when there's an issue to resolve.
  6. Recordings. Kept while someone is a member, and deleted when they leave.
  7. A person checks reports. Findings in a report, including those from asking members live, are checked by a person before a client sees them. Instant answers from our data come straight to you, with their sources shown.

How members tell us things

  • Surveys.
  • Voice notes and video diaries, in their own words.
  • Secure on-screen review, with download and screenshots blocked.
  • Live interviews and groups.

Equity Engine, our member platform, also gives members community and content, which keeps them coming back.

What the AI never does

  • Invent patients. No synthetic patients, and no outside data filling the gaps.
  • Answer without showing its working. Every answer traces back to real members.
  • Identify anyone. Names and anything that could identify a member are removed before any text is analysed.
