The Exclusion Debt Report

A monthly report on exclusion debt: the cost of building medical evidence without the people it is about. Each issue takes one disease and asks a single question across the whole evidence chain, from clinical trial design to screening to the datasets that train AI: who is missing, and what does it cost?

About the report

Exclusion debt is what accumulates when evidence about a disease is built without the people who have it. It's created at specific moments, the design of a clinical trial, the setting of a screening threshold, the assembly of a dataset, and it can't be undone after the fact. Each issue reads every gap the same way: what evidence exists, what is missing, what it means for patients, and how severe the gap is.

The report is a synthesis of published evidence, and every figure is drawn from the sources it lists. More on exclusion debt
