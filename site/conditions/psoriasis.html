Our members · Psoriasis

900 people living with psoriasis have joined us.

Across the UK, and growing daily. For clinical trials and research in psoriasis, we count who we hold near your sites the same day, put your protocol and materials in front of them before they're final, and refer the ones who pass your screening questions and say yes, following every referral through to randomisation.

In the most deprived fifth of areas

Counted September 2026. Each member is counted once, under the main condition they told us about. Deprivation is worked out from members' postcodes using the Index of Multiple Deprivation; an even spread would put about 20% in the most deprived fifth of areas. Every member has finished onboarding and passed our sign-up checks, and conditions are self-declared, with qualifying questions.

Psoriasis in the UK

2% to 3%

of the UK population is estimated to have psoriasis, up to about 1.8 million people.

Psoriasis UK (formerly the Psoriasis Association), About psoriasis.

Grey, purple or brown

On brown or black skin, psoriasis patches can look grey, purple or dark brown, and can leave dark marks after they clear.

NHS, Psoriasis, reviewed March 2026; Psoriasis UK, Plaque psoriasis, reviewed August 2026.

What you can ask for

  1. A free panel count, the same working day. How many members with psoriasis we hold near your UK sites, by postcode district, and how they break down. Free panel count
  2. Patient involvement, before it's final. Members with psoriasis review your protocol, participant information sheet, consent form or product, and you get a report of what changed. Patient involvement
  3. Referrals, followed through. Members who pass your screening questions and say yes are referred to the site, and we follow each one to randomisation. Clinical trial recruitment in the UK
  4. Ask our data, then members live. An answer straight away from what members have already told us, linked to the members who gave it. Ask our data
An example question

"Which changes matter most to people with psoriasis when they judge whether a treatment is working?"

What comes back

An answer straight away from what members with psoriasis have already told us, linked to the members who said it. Then ask members live: survey answers, voice notes and video diaries within hours or days, broken down by age, sex, ethnicity and deprivation, with the themes, how many raised each one and who.
