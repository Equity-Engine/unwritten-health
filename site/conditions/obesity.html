Our members · Obesity

1,400 people living with obesity have joined us.

In the UK, and growing daily, built from the communities clinical trials miss. For clinical trials in obesity, we tell you the same day how many members near your sites match and have already said yes, put your protocol and materials in front of them before the ethics submission, and refer the ones who pass your screening questions and say yes, following every referral through to randomisation.

What you can ask for

  1. Free patient feasibility, the same working day. How many members with obesity near your UK sites match and have already said yes, by postcode district, and how they break down. Pre-screened, never eligible. Free patient feasibility
  2. Patient engagement, before the ethics submission. Members with obesity review your protocol, participant information sheet, consent form or product, and you get a report of what changed. Patient engagement
  3. Referrals, followed through. Members who pass your screening questions and say yes are referred through the site's own referral link, and we follow each one to randomisation. Clinical trial recruitment in the UK
  4. Ask our data, then ask members live. An answer straight away from what members have already told us, linked to the members who gave it. Ask our data
An example question

"How do people living with obesity want a weight management study described to them?"

What comes back

An answer straight away from what members with obesity have already told us, linked to the members who said it. Then ask members live: survey answers, voice notes and video diaries within hours or days, broken down by age, sex, ethnicity and deprivation, with the themes, how many raised each one and who.
